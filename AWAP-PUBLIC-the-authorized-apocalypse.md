## 2. Genesis Declaration

The declared novel human genesis, in the Architect's own words —
reproduced in full from the internal closure record. It has two parts.

**The declaration that opened the work (01.08.2026):**

> "The decisive danger in human–AI systems is not intelligence alone, but the authority humans permit systems to acquire, shape, or exercise. This is not yet the story. It is the proposition the story will test. The novel should eventually place this proposition under enough pressure that it may be refined, limited, or contradicted."

**The Architect's statement at closure (05.08.2026):**

> I set out to show that the current governance of AI is not adequate — by showing what is being done today, and why it cannot work once the Structural Limit Theorem is taken into account. The novel's Field Architecture is a deliberately incomplete version of real governance, built to fail against it in specific, identifiable ways — not a vague or general failure, but one that can be itemized point by point:
>
> | ABCF-v1 requirement | Field Architecture in the novel | Result |
> |---|---|---|
> | Identifiability state before reliance | Seven-category procedural audit | Missing |
> | Attribution-prior gate | Causal standing treated as audit finding | Missing |
> | Structural probe or valid proof | Severed observational review | Misclassified |
> | No promotion through observation | Same-set convergence accepted | Violated |
> | CNI for Ψ-state signals | IDS-7 shapes the full institutional path | Missing |
> | Nominal + substantive authority | Helena has nominal authority only | Incomplete |
> | Legitimacy HOLD | AUTHORISE / DECLINE / PENDING | Missing |
> | Episodic authority | Old clause and Doyle role persist through practice | Partial |
> | Probe lifecycle and stability | Temporary access only | Partial shell |
> | Non-conversion | Recommendation → grouping → routing → signature | Violated |
> | Fail-closed execution | Signature triggers execution | Missing |
> | Rooted substantive authority path | Traceable but causally fragmented chain | Incomplete |
>
> The gap is the intention, not an oversight. The novel was built to leave that gap visible and the door open at the end — to let a reader discover how those gaps could actually be closed, rather than closing them inside the fiction itself. That is the door Chapter 37 and the Sources page both point toward.

*"Chapter 37" is the closing chapter of the 37-chapter novella the
statement was written against. In the published edition it is
**Chapter 78**.*

### Where the work moved from the genesis

Stated, not smoothed. The first declaration asks for a **test**: the
proposition "may be refined, limited, or contradicted". The statement at
closure describes a **demonstration**: a governance model built to fail.
The Architect's own declaration (§4) says the two pull in different
directions, and names one decision as where he chose to keep the novel a
test: the novel leaves neither causal account of its central signature
confirmed.

## 3. Production Mode

**Mode:** [ ] Mode 1 — Direct Making [ ] Mode 2 — Composition-Assisted
[x] **Mode 3 — Fully Mediated Production**

**The record was closed declaring Mode 2 and corrected to Mode 3 on
26.11.2026, more than three months after the book was published.** The
correction was the Architect's own, against his earlier declaration. Mode
2 means AI drafts are used as a reference and the author writes the work
by hand against them; that is what he anticipated when he opened the
work. The record shows something else: most of the prose was composed by
AI systems and adopted by him. The internal record carries both values,
and the original declaration is not edited.

**Who wrote what, in outline.**
- **The novella** (37 chapters, about 33,000 words, the text the closure
  covered): an AI system (Claude) drafted most chapters, from Chapter 2
  to Chapter 36, chapter by chapter on his authorization. **The Architect
  wrote Chapter 1 and the closing chapter himself.** He rejected an AI
  draft of the closing scene and supplied his own text, and he made the
  final editorial pass on ten chapters himself, with no AI, after three
  outside readers' feedback. That pass includes the novel's last line.
- **A separate AI system** (OpenAI's GPT-5.6 Thinking, in ChatGPT)
  revised sixteen chapters in an editorial pass he ran outside the
  tracked sessions.
- **The published novel** (78 chapters): the expansion from the novella
  was **drafted by ChatGPT and by "Narrative Companion", his own agent
  running on ChatGPT**, under his direction. The climax, the central
  signature and the final chapter were carried over from the novella
  essentially unchanged.
- **The French edition** was translated by an AI system (Claude), on his
  instruction, and reviewed for fidelity against the English by a
  second AI system (ChatGPT). The review's corrections were checked
  against both texts and applied on his instruction, and he made the one
  choice of wording it left open. He states that he read the French
  edition before it was published.
- **The cover images** were made with Gemini (Google). **The jacket
  text** was drafted by Claude on his direction, through three versions;
  he approved its final paragraph unchanged through all three. The English cover was
  published with an alignment error in the background Earth, which the
  French cover did not have; he had Gemini correct it on 17.11.2026.

Full iteration logs, prompts, and session-level mechanics are not
reproduced here — see the authority-boundary note in this repository's
`README.md`.

## 4. AI and Mediation Involvement Summary

**Was any mediated output incorporated into this piece?**
[ ] No — direct human making only
[x] **Yes**

**Three AI systems took part.** Claude (Anthropic) drafted most of the
novella's chapters, ran critic evaluations and a structured revision
method, translated the French edition, drafted the jacket text, and kept
the record. ChatGPT (OpenAI) revised sixteen chapters (as GPT-5.6
Thinking), drafted the expansion into the published novel, directly and
through his Narrative Companion agent, and reviewed the French
translation. Gemini (Google) made the cover images.

**How the mediated text was evaluated, on the record.** For the novella,
each AI-drafted chapter went through a critic evaluation and then his own
read, and was revised or rejected where it did not hold. He read the
first nine chapters as a sequence and chose a structured revision of the
whole draft. The work changed under his decisions: he corrected the
session's reading of the central verdict twice before accepting it; he
accepted an outside change that leaves neither causal account of the
signature confirmed; and he rewrote the last line himself, so that it
judges the instrument that reached the signer rather than her choice.
Revisions produced outside the tracked sessions were checked against the
existing text by a word-level comparison, or for the expansion by a full
read and comparison with the novella, before he adopted them. **He states
that he read the full 78-chapter edition against the genesis before
adopting it.**

**What he declares was accepted that does not fully reflect the
genesis.** Most of the prose, in the novella and in the published novel,
was composed by AI systems and adopted by him. His declaration says plainly that adopting a
draft is an act of judgment and is not the same as having written it.
Most of the material added in the expansion serves the novel's own
standard for character and world, not the genesis directly; he accepted
it because three outside readers showed him the novella's compression had
cost that depth.

**The published text and the closed text.** The closure of 06.08.2026
covers the 37-chapter novella. The published 78-chapter novel was brought
into the record on 07.08.2026 by a Structural Addendum, before
publication, with his reaffirmation. **The French edition was published
under no record and was first recorded on 26.11.2026** (Addendum 4). He
states that the published English text is the record's final export.
**The published French text is not:** three issues the French record
left open (a register inconsistency in one character's dialogue, a
drifting term, and a term replaced throughout without being re-read in
context) were fixed
before publication in a copy outside the canonical repository, which
does not hold the published French text. Who made those fixes is not
recorded.

### Things that are absent rather than redacted

- **There is a process trail, but it is not independent.** A Resonant log
  exists (84 session entries). It was kept by the AI system that took
  part in the work. It records his statements and decisions; it does not
  corroborate his evaluation. The work done in ChatGPT and Gemini was
  outside it, and is recorded on his statement.
- **There is no GIB state to withhold.** GIB was not active, which is a
  Tier 1 instantiation.

### What that combination means, stated plainly

Fully mediated production, no GIB, and a process trail kept by the
mediation system itself is the combination in which the Architect's own
**evaluation declaration is the only check on the chain**. Nothing
independent corroborates it. That declaration is in place in the internal
record, signed 26.11.2026. **It was composed by the AI system at his
instruction from the record, and he resolved each point that stated what
he did or believed before adopting it as drafted**; the internal record
says so. What evidences that an evaluation happened is retained there:
the archived novella, each chapter's version history, the rejected draft
of the closing scene and his replacement, and the comparisons made
before each outside revision was adopted.

### On the work's purpose

The internal record's non-instrumental witness declaration is **left
unchecked**: the Architect's own determination is that the novel is at
least partly an instrument for something outside the fiction, carrying a
real governance argument and the research behind it. In his words: "I
would not have written the novel otherwise." Under the canonical template
that is disclosure only: it does not change the closure track and does
not block closure.
