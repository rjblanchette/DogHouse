## How this record works

This series is **one Work of nine essays, closed once.** The internal
record, `SDCA-AWAP-SUBSTACK-004`, was closed on 10.11.2026, when only the
first essay had been published. It covers all nine essays. Each later
publication is recorded against that same closure as a §8a addendum and
is not a new closure.

So this folder holds **one** public record for the whole series, and not
one file per essay. The sections below that describe the Work (§§1–6) were
written once, at closure, and are not edited when an essay is published.
Two parts change as the series proceeds, and only these two:

- the **table of essays** (§7), which gains a row when an essay is published; and
- the **changelog**, at the end, which records every change to this file.

Essay 1's own public AWAP,
[`AWAP-PUBLIC-can-a-machine-describe-feeling-without-feeling-anything.md`](../../AWAP-PUBLIC-can-a-machine-describe-feeling-without-feeling-anything.md),
was filed first and stays where it is, because published posts link to it.
It carries an amendment pointing here.

## 2. Genesis Declaration

The declared novel human genesis, in the Architect's own words —
reproduced in full from the internal closure record. This is the
Work-level genesis; no separate genesis was declared for individual
essays within it.

> A nine-part essay series — "What Does It Mean to Feel? Consciousness,
> Conscience, and the Possibility of Artificial Experience" — working
> through, essay by essay, what is actually known, what is assumed,
> and what remains unresolved about the physical and biological
> conditions for phenomenal experience: from access vs. phenomenal
> consciousness, through locked-in syndrome and covert awareness,
> personal identity under brain replacement, memory versus felt
> significance, grief and the difference between representing loss
> and suffering it, consciousness without moral conscience, brain-body
> coupling under artificial organ support, and what would or would not
> establish that a non-organic system actually feels anything — closing
> (essay 9) on why phenomenal consciousness, moral conscience, legal
> personhood, and institutional authority must stay strictly separated.

## 4. AI and Mediation Involvement Summary

**Was any mediated output incorporated into this Work?**
[ ] No — direct human making only
[x] **Yes**

**The essays.** The topic of each of the nine essays was determined by
ChatGPT during the opening session of that production. ChatGPT wrote the
full prose of all nine essays — both the original drafts and the
subsequent revision pass. The Architect directed and guided that process
throughout but did not himself select the topics or write any of the
text. Academic citations were added to each essay in a separate,
independently-verified pass before export, and are appended at the end of
each published essay rather than inline.

**This production-method account is Reconciled, not Native.** It is
recorded in the internal closure record's CEA-1, filed after the fact —
the decision and the ChatGPT session it describes happened entirely
outside any session the private repository tracks. Verification
consisted of confirming that the drafted and revised files exist as
described; no session transcript was available to check the account
against independently. The internal record treats the Architect's own
direct statement as the authoritative source for this kind of
production-method declaration, consistent with SDCA's Human
Responsibility framing. No external work-time record exists for the
originating ChatGPT session.

**One exception, added after closure.** A second conception record
(CEA-2, filed as *Native* and confirmed by the Architect on 20.11.2026)
records five passages added to **essays 8 and 9**: one to essay 8 and four
to essay 9. Those passages were drafted by Claude (Anthropic), run as a
coding/agent session, and approved one by one by the Architect, who chose
which to add. They are not ChatGPT's prose, and CEA-1's account does not
describe them. CEA-2 does not touch essays 1 to 7.

**The images.** Each essay's post carries an image. Each was conceived by
the Architect and generated with Gemini, by his statement of 18.11.2026
(he named the tool as "Gemini 4 Carbon"). The prompts and the dates they
were made are not recorded, and the images are not covered by either CEA
record filed to date.
