## 2. Genesis Declaration

The declared novel human genesis, in the Architect's own words —
reproduced in full from the internal closure record. This is the
Work-level genesis; no separate genesis was declared for individual
essays within it.

> A nine-part essay series — "What Does It Mean to Feel? Consciousness,
> Conscience, and the Possibility of Artificial Experience" — working
> through, essay by essay, what is actually known, what is assumed,
> and what remains unresolved about the physical and biological
> conditions for phenomenal experience: from access vs. phenomenal
> consciousness, through locked-in syndrome and covert awareness,
> personal identity under brain replacement, memory versus felt
> significance, grief and the difference between representing loss
> and suffering it, consciousness without moral conscience, brain-body
> coupling under artificial organ support, and what would or would not
> establish that a non-organic system actually feels anything — closing
> (essay 9) on why phenomenal consciousness, moral conscience, legal
> personhood, and institutional authority must stay strictly separated.
