## 2. Genesis Declaration

The declared novel human genesis, in the Architect's own words —
reproduced in full from the internal closure record.

> The article is intended to examine what happens when the structural
> attribution problem is carried into ordinary economic questions about
> AI.
>
> In particular, the work asks:
>
> - what can actually be recognized as technological surplus;
> - why an observable proxy does not automatically identify the source
>   of that surplus;
> - how the baseline and counterfactual problem changes what can
>   legitimately be claimed;
> - why outsourcing and contractor substitution matter to labour-time
>   measurement;
> - why recognition and later capture are separate questions;
> - and what these distinctions may mean for labour, bargaining, social
>   protection and taxation if automation reduces necessary human
>   labour.
>
> The work is explanatory and exploratory. It does not claim to settle
> the later policy choices.

### The origin gate was declared late

This genesis declaration is **retrospective**. It was made on
2026-09-05, after the discussion, drafting, redrafting and voice

states this plainly rather than presenting the declaration as

Under SDCA, a Declared-Origin Gate opens a genesis chain and qualifies
